HOW TO PLAY

🕹️
TOUCH & DRAG STEERING

Drag anywhere on screen (or use WASD keys) to steer Patient Zero and command the flocking horde through avenues and intersections.

🏹
SWARM SQUEEZE & TRANSFER

Hold the SQUEEZE button (or C / Shift) to compress your horde into a tight spearhead through narrow alleys. Swipe flick (or E) transfers Patient Zero to a forward zombie!

🧟
INFECTION & RECRUITMENT

Touch civilians to convert them into your horde. Chain rapid infections to rack up combo multipliers, and recruit stray roaming zombies to rebuild numbers.

⚠️
HAZMAT CONES & LAST STAND

Hazmat spray decontaminates zombies! Horde units shield Patient Zero. If your horde is wiped out, you enter Last Stand: infect a civilian before mist or the countdown eliminates you!

⚡
POWER-UP CANISTERS

Collect street canisters: ⚡ Speed Surge for hyper-speed, 🧲 Meat Magnet to vacuum civilians from afar, and ☣️ Titan Virus for colossal stomping invulnerability!
